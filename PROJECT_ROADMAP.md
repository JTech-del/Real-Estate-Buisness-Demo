# Project Roadmap

## Real Estate Business Demo

This roadmap defines the development stages, current project status, deployment goals, and future evolution of the Real Estate Business Demo.

The project is intentionally developed in progressive stages rather than attempting to build a complete real-estate platform at once.

---

# 1. Roadmap Philosophy

The project follows a progressive development model:

```text
Planning
   ↓
Frontend Foundation
   ↓
Business MVP
   ↓
Production Hardening
   ↓
Deployment
   ↓
Validation
   ↓
Future Backend
   ↓
Production Platform
